A clear path to a ready close
This is Odin Close Control, an independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. On the public app, I'll take one fictional deal from a cash exception to a reviewed handoff.

01 · Upload commitments
First, I upload the commitments CSV: known synthetic accounts and exact amounts.

02 · Upload payment events
Then the payment events CSV. I confirm that these are the supplied synthetic records and ask for a preview.

Preview before saving
The server shows commitment changes and new payment events before anything is saved. The preview is tied to this revision.

Apply one atomic import
I apply these validated files together. Either every change is saved, or none is.

Keep the exception visible
Aurora still has a two thousand pound receipt without an account. It stays outside matched cash until someone resolves it.

Choose the exact account
I choose Linden Ventures' commitment for this receipt, then explicitly confirm the match. That decision is recorded in the audit trail.

Cash reconciled
The cash shortfall is now zero. Principal and illustrative fees stay separate. Final legal evidence is still required.

Attach a final reference
I attach the final synthetic legal metadata. It records a document reference and hash; it does not verify a real agreement.

Request human review
The readiness checks are met. I request a human review of this saved revision.

Review this exact snapshot
The synthetic reviewer checks this exact snapshot and explicitly approves it. A relevant later change would clear that approval.

Reload the saved review
I reload the public app. The approved revision is still present in the saved session.

Download the reviewed packet
Now I download the actual packet JSON and cash ledger. The packet contains the approved revision and its snapshot hash.

An inspectable human handoff
This is a reviewed readiness handoff. It does not sign an agreement, close an investment, or move funds.
