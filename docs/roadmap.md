# Roadmap

**Ayo Ahmed · independent synthetic concept**

I separate the candidacy demonstration from a possible production programme. The latter depends on Odin confirming the workflow, controls and economic case. These phases are decision gates, not promised delivery dates or approved work.

| Phase | Outcome | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — public grounding | Establish a documented closing workflow and inspect the current role and visual language. | Captured official sources, source manifest and brand evidence. No claim of internal discovery. |
| 1 — synthetic concept | Demonstrate previewed atomic intake, exact cash/fee reconciliation, visible exceptions, human attestations and current-revision review/export across three fictional deals. | Reproducible domain/API tests and browser evidence, with limitations recorded. |
| 2 — discovery with Odin | Confirm the actual handoff, source systems, definitions, permissions, exception causes and ownership. | Agreed process map, control requirements and measured baseline; a decision to proceed, change the scope or stop. |
| 3 — controlled shadow trial | Compare packets with the existing authorised workflow without letting this system initiate closing actions. | Like-for-like handling and review measurements, explained discrepancies, no unresolved control regression and an agreed operating owner. |
| 4 — bounded live adoption | Replace a specific duplicate preparation task using approved integrations and identities. | Approved access/retention policy, reconciliation to authoritative sources, recovery procedure, monitoring and demonstrated net benefit. |
| 5 — justified extension | Expand only into an adjacent job whose need and cost have been measured. | A separately scoped business and control case. No automatic expansion into Odin's other processes. |

## My first discovery questions

I would watch one normal deal and one exception reach the handoff, then identify where evidence is re-entered or checked again. I would ask the team to define collected funds, final legals and close request precisely; show which changes reopen review; and explain when overpayments, FX, fees, refunds or reversals require specialist decisions. I would confirm actual roles and whether preparer/reviewer separation is mandatory.

I would also ask who owns unresolved items, which dependencies determine elapsed closing time and which customer communications must remain with the Deal Lead. Those answers could change both the proposed readiness rules and the interface. No interviews or shadow cases have been performed for this concept.

## What I defer

I defer live bank feeds, real investor identity, FX conversion, fee-policy integration, automatic legal assessment, messaging, reminders, forecasting and support for additional operational processes. I leave KYC/AML decisions, document signing and wires with authorised humans and systems in every proposed phase. An approved readiness packet is always distinct from confirmation that an investment has completed.

## Why the gates matter

The demo can establish whether my controls work on synthetic inputs. Discovery establishes whether they solve the actual job. A shadow trial establishes whether they reduce net work without weakening controls. Adoption requires an owned system with trustworthy data and recovery. I would use the [metrics plan](metrics.md) at each gate and remove work that fails to earn its place.
