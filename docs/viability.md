## Why this is a plausible wedge

The closing and drafting guides establish a clear dependency chain: funds collected and a Deal Lead close request precede Financial Operations closing; final-legals availability and wire arrival affect completion; fee payer changes investable capital. That makes a reconciliation and readiness handoff a specific problem I can model without inventing Odin's private processes.

The role posting says Odin is on track for roughly 700 deals a year and aims for 7,000 with a team of around 50. I treat those as Odin's stated trajectory and ambition, not verified outcomes. Repeated preparation work could become expensive at that scale, but I need measured handling effort and exception frequency before claiming that this concept reduces the denominator.

## Commercial and operational case to test

I would propose adoption only if the system replaces repeated work and does not create a second ledger. The economic hypothesis is:

`benefit = avoided measured handling/rework cost − added measured review, integration, maintenance and hosting cost`

There is no value attached to that equation in this submission because there is no real baseline. A technically correct prototype can still be a poor investment if data integration is expensive, exceptions require excessive specialist judgment or the existing system already does this well.

The synthetic application uses session-isolated state and a small deterministic rules layer. A live service would require an authorised identity model, approved data access, source-system integration, durable evidence retention, operating ownership and incident recovery. Demo reviewer labels and a public sample session are not sufficient production controls. Deployment status and actual platform limits should be taken from the deployment evidence, not assumed from a platform's free tier.

## Conditions for proceeding

I would first ask Odin to confirm that the handoff exists as modelled, which checks are necessary, who can attest and approve, what evidence proves collected funds, what tolerances and fee rules apply, and which records can change after review. I would then run the concept in shadow mode against representative authorised cases, with existing controls remaining responsible for decisions.

I would stop or reshape the work if it merely duplicates a reliable source of truth, fails to reduce net handling effort, makes exception ownership less clear or cannot meet the required control standard. I would extend it only after those conditions are answered. This is the scope discipline I want the candidacy to demonstrate.
